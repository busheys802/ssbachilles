WkWork
Never, while you have this
× Heel drops off a step   × Calf stretching against a wall   × Slant boards   × Zero-drop shoes   × Digging into the sore spot   × Cortisone into the tendon
All of these pull your toes toward your shin, which crushes the tendon against your heel bone. That compression is the injury.
Also
Heel lifts, 10–12mm, both shoes, including work shoes. Down dog with knees bent and heels high, or take child's pose. Bike with the pedal spindle nearer mid-foot, seated, 85–95rpm — in addition to running, not instead of it. Avoid the elliptical and stair climber; your foot sits in sustained dorsiflexion under load.
Stop and get seen
A pop or sudden sharp pain · you can't do a single-leg heel raise · a visible gap in the tendon · the foot feels weak or floppy · redness and heat with fever · numbness or pins and needles.
